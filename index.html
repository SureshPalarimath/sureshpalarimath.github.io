ABOUT

Teaching, research and innovation.

Dr. Suresh Palarimath is an Information Technology academic and researcher with international teaching experience and a strong interest in applying emerging technologies to meaningful educational and real-world problems.

His work brings together Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, IoT, intelligent systems, digital transformation and AI-enabled education. His academic interests also include inclusive and personalised learning, explainable AI, computer vision and smart campus technologies.

He is particularly interested in research that moves beyond prototypes and connects technology with practical impact, sustainability and human-centred outcomes.
