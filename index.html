    In brief

    I have taught computer science for more than 18 years across Oman, Papua New Guinea and India, at diploma, undergraduate and postgraduate levels. I have served as Head of School and Head of Department, and I currently coordinate the Software Engineering and Data Science programme at UTAS Salalah and chair its Research and Consultancy Committee.

    I received the UTAS Salalah Best Researcher Award in 2024 and 2025, and I am an IEEE Senior Member. I hold a PhD in Computer Science and completed a postdoctoral fellowship at Lincoln University College, Malaysia.
